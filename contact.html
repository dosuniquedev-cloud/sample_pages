Before you write

The four questions the desk answers most often.

How quickly will I hear back?

During desk hours, most enquiries get a first reply within two hours. Anything sent overnight is answered the next morning — always by a planner, never a template.

Can you build a fully custom itinerary?

Yes, and it is most of what we do. Share your dates, group and interests, and we will design a private route with stays, transfers and guides quoted separately so nothing is hidden.

What are the payment and cancellation terms?

A 30% deposit confirms dates, with the balance due 15 days before departure. Changes are free up to 15 days out; cancellations within 7 days retain the deposit. Full terms are sent with every quote.

Do you arrange pick-ups from Kolkata or Berhampore?

We do — station and airport transfers are part of every itinerary we quote, including the early-morning departures for the hills and the Sundarbans.

Still deciding where to go?

Bring us a month and a mood. We will bring the map.
